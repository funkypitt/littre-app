Le Littré is an offline Android edition of Émile Littré's Dictionnaire de la langue française (Paris, Hachette, 1873-1874), based on the free XML data of François Gannaz (littre.org, CC BY-SA 3.0).

Features:
- 78,599 dictionary entries, fully available offline
- Instant prefix search (autocomplete)
- Full-text search inside the definitions
- Word of the day, with a home-screen widget
- Favourites and consultation history
- Clickable cross-references between articles
- Automatic dark mode
- Careful typography (Merriweather)

The original text by Émile Littré is in the public domain.
